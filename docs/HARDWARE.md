# Hardware Requirements

## Minimum
- 64 bit Intel or AMD processor with at least 2 cores
- 4 GB RAM
- 20 GB free storage
- 1024 × 768 display
- 4 GB USB drive

## Recommended
- Modern 64 bit Intel or AMD processor
- 8 GB RAM or more
- 40 GB or more SSD storage
- Full HD display
- 8 GB or larger USB drive

These requirements apply to DeshiOS 26.08 RC1.
